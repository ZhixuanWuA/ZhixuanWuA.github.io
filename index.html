👋 Hello! I'm Zhixuan.

I am a PhD candidate at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, supervised by Prof. Cheng Bo (Director, Basic Research Center for Network Services, State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology). I am also a visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, working with Assoc. Prof. Soujanya Poria (DeCLaRe Lab Leader, H-index 88).

My research lies at the intersection of machine vision, multi-modal understanding, and interactive cognition, with a focus on building AI systems that can perceive, reason, and interact in complex environments.
